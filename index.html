Privacy Policy for Colorblind Style

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Overview

Colorblind Style is a color matching app designed to help people with color vision deficiency confidently match clothing. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Camera Access

Colorblind Style requires access to your device's camera to identify colors from clothing items. Camera images are processed entirely on your device and are never transmitted to our servers or any third party. We do not store, record, or retain any photos or video from your camera.

Data Storage

Colorblind Style stores your saved color matches and color identifications locally on your device using your device's built-in storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Personal information of any kind
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Analytics or usage data
  • Any information that could identify you

Third Party Services

Colorblind Style does not use any third party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

Data Deletion

You can delete all data stored by Colorblind Style at any time by deleting the app from your device.

Children's Privacy

Colorblind Style does not collect any personal information from anyone including children under the age of 13.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy please contact us at webdevtodd@gmail.com

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the date at the top of this policy.
